{% comment %} The player's own war band, one line per man, in columns he can compare down. What the line carries is what decides a month: level, renown, strength and dexterity, health and morale, the gear he stands in, his wage, and whether he can fight. Everything else - culture, injuries, experience - is on his own page, which the name leads to. Renown is here because it decides who takes the leader's seat if he falls, and that is a question asked down the column: how far behind the first man is the second. Strength and dexterity are the stored figures, the ones his own page and the captive table show, each against the strength baseline because the two are drawn from one distribution. Health and morale are a bar and the figure beside it. The bar is against his own maximum, because the column asks how hurt he is, and it goes "blood" below a third, which is where a man is one bad blow from dropping. The figure is still the gauge component, so the numbers stay governed by docs/patterns/warrior-knowledge.md even though only his own men are ever drawn here. The status cell is empty for a man who can fight. That is the normal state, and a tag on five rows out of six would be the loudest thing in the table while saying nothing. On a phone each man is a record - see docs/patterns/responsive-layout.md: the name across the top with the row menu beside it, the short figures two to a line, the gauges and the gear one to a line. On a desk narrower than the table it scrolls inside its own box rather than dragging the page sideways. The box is positioned so that it contains the visually hidden column heads and the row menu: an absolutely positioned element escapes an overflow box that is not its containing block, and that one would widen the page. {% endcomment %}
