Renown is public and exact. It is the figure that decides where a recruited man stands in the line of succession, and a rival's fallen leader can carry enough of it to go straight to the front. Held rather than commanded: the numbers arrive fuzzed and the gear is named, as docs/patterns/warrior-knowledge.md has it for a prisoner, so the gauge draws the word and no bar. The wage is public, and it is the one recruiting him commits the player to every month after - a leader draws nothing while he leads, and is put on a wage the moment he is taken on. A rival's cells keep the row, the way a rival's roster does - see "captured_warrior_list.html". A record per man on a phone and a box that scrolls on a narrow desk, for the reasons "roster_table.html" gives. {% endcomment %}
