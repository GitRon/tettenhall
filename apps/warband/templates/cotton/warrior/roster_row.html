{% comment %} One man as a line of "". The row menu is the slot: what can be done to a man of the war band and to a prisoner in the cells are different things. What the line carries is what decides a month: level, renown, strength and dexterity, health and morale, the gear he stands in, his wage, and whether he can fight. Everything else - culture, injuries, experience - is on his own page, which the name leads to. Renown is here because it decides who takes the leader's seat if he falls, and that is a question asked down the column: how far behind the first man is the second. For a prisoner it is the figure that decides where he would stand in that line once recruited, and a rival's fallen leader can carry enough of it to go straight to the front. Strength and dexterity are the stored figures, each against the strength baseline because the two are drawn from one distribution. For a man the player does not yet command they are most of what recruiting him would buy. Health and morale follow "knowledge": a bar and the exact figure for his own men, the fuzzed word and no bar for a prisoner - see "" and docs/patterns/warrior-knowledge.md. "wage" is the caller's: what the man is paid, or what taking him on would put him on. The leader's frame is the brightest rule, and "unpaid_note" says why a man's morale is stuck - the monthly sweep skips a man carrying unpaid months, so he sits below his ceiling while every other warrior refills for free, and without it the player reads the low figure and no cause. The status cell is empty for a man who can fight. That is the normal state, and a tag on five rows out of six would be the loudest thing in the table while saying nothing. {% endcomment %}
