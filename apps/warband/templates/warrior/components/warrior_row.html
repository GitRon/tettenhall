{% load static %} {% comment %} One man, as a row: the lists that show somebody else's warriors side by side - a rival's roster, the captives a rival holds, the mercenaries in a pub. The player's own war band and his own cells are tables instead, "faction/warrior/components/roster_table.html" and "captive_table.html", because those are the men he decides about and compares down a column. What the row carries is what decides a month: level, health and morale, wage, the gear he stands in, and whether he can fight. Everything else - culture, strength and dexterity, injuries, what a blade rolls - is one click away on his own page, which the name leads to and which answers every relation this row is rendered for. What may be known is not this template's to decide: "knowledge" arrives from the list, and the gauge and the gear follow it - exact figures for his own men, a bucket for everybody else, and no gear at all for a rival's. See docs/patterns/warrior-knowledge.md. "is_pub" says the list is a pub, where "is_player_faction" decides whether there is anything to press. Nothing else drawn as a row has a control. A phone gets the avatar and the name on one line, the tags across the whole row below, and the controls under them - squeezed beside a control, the tags stack one to a line. A desk puts the controls on the name's line. Set left explicitly: a rival's page centres the column it sits in. {% endcomment %}
